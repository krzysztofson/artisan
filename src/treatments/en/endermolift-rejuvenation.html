The goals of Endermolift® facial treatments are:

  • brightening
  • filling
  • firming
  • skin densification

Endermolift™ is a non-invasive technique that addresses the main causes of skin aging and loss of skin density.

The patient undergoes a painless treatment in individually tailored series. LPG Lifts delivers micro-impulses to the skin surface, which then penetrate deeper, triggering a biological response from fibroblasts and reactivating the natural production of collagen and elastin. Endermolift™ improves venous-lymphatic circulation and removes fibrous barriers to facilitate tissue oxygenation. It is designed for thin, aging skin showing signs of sagging. Endermolift® uses the patented LIFT Technology, which works through the synergistic action of two flaps in the treatment chamber of the handpiece and sequential skin suction to stimulate peripheral tissue.
