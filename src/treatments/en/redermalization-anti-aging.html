--- id: redermalizacja-nowy-zabieg-anti-aging lead: Redermalization is a new method of anti-aging prevention and skin renewal. Through the use of innovative Xela Rederm® preparations by the Swiss company Hyalual, containing sodium succinate and hyaluronic acid, the biochemical and physiological processes in cells are regulated. The procedure involves injection of the preparations (using the mesotherapy method) and effectively blocks the formation of free radicals and prevents skin dehydration. Redermalization is applied to the face, neck, décolletage, hands and under the eyes. ---

Xela Rederm® preparations contain sodium succinate and hyaluronic acid at concentrations of 1.1%, 1.8% or 2.2%. Sodium succinate is the active ingredient of the preparations, while hyaluronic acid supports its action by restoring the water balance and providing the right environment for fibroblast activity.

Xela Rederm® is the only product on the market that has a direct effect on the metabolic processes in the skin (increased energy production in the Krebs cycle in skin fibroblasts, improved cellular respiration, ion transport, synthesis of collagen, elastin and proteins, increased microcirculation). Thanks to these properties, cell viability increases and greater protection against damage is ensured.

Indications for redermalization:

Correction and prevention of changes in skin condition caused by:

  • skin ageing
  • pathological environmental factors (UV radiation, environmental pollution, smoking, etc.)
  • side effects of certain aesthetic medicine procedures (scars, pigmentation)

Redermalization is an excellent method for the correction and prevention of age-related changes. It allows the restoration of skin structure and quality, while improving morphological and functional characteristics such as elasticity, suppleness and hydration level. Redermalization treatments slow down skin ageing not only through proper hydration, but also through their influence on the processes regulating energy production in skin cells.
