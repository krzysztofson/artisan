It has long been known that women dream of being fully satisfied with their appearance. A particularly difficult and uneven battle is fought against “rolls” in areas where fat does not disappear despite regular exercise and diet, such as: hips, saddlebags or the inner side of the knees. Until now, the only way to remove fat in these areas was liposuction. Now an alternative to this procedure is Aqualyx — a non-surgical and express method of removing adipose tissue. The new Aqualyx preparation is the only registered product in the world for injectable adipose tissue removal procedures. Aqualyx is a solution that dissolves fat and destroys cell membranes; injected into adipose tissue, it causes the destruction of fat cells, which are then transported to the liver and excreted from the body via the kidneys. The Aqualyx procedure is painless and minimally invasive. Thanks to the limited number of injections (1–2 punctures), this method significantly reduces the risk of bruising, skin damage, and also reduces pain during the procedure itself.

Indications for the procedure:

  • Local excess adipose tissue.
