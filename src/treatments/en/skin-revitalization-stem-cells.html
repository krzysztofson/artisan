--- id: rewitalizacja-skory-komorkami-macierzystymi lead: Stem cells enable the skin to regain its freshness and vitality. Treatments using them for skin revitalisation deliver excellent results. ---

The method involves harvesting adipose tissue from a selected area and subjecting it to enzymatic digestion. This yields a suspension containing millions of stem cells, which can then be administered via mesotherapy to the areas requiring treatment.

The procedure is minimally invasive; patients can return to normal activities within 24 hours. Results are excellent and long-lasting.
