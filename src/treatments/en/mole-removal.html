--- id: wyciecie-znamienia lead: Moles are often unsightly or bothersome, and are also susceptible to mechanical damage (e.g. along clothing lines, etc.). This simple procedure allows patients to regain confidence and comfort. ---

Indications:

  • Mole / nevus in a location that limits function,
  • Mole / nevus qualified for excision following dermoscopic examination, etc.

Before the surgery:

Determining the location and size of the mole.

Type of anesthesia:

Local.

Duration of the surgery:

Depending on the size of the mole (approx. 40–60 min).

Course of the surgery:

Anesthesia. Excision. Layered wound closure.

Postoperative recommendations:

  • Change dressing with clean hands
  • Disinfect the operated area (wound) with a slightly moist gauze pad soaked in Octanisept solution or salicylic alcohol
  • Do not wet the wound for the first 4 days
  • Do not wash the area where strips/plasters are applied with soap, as this may cause the plasters to come off
  • Suture removal on day 5–10 (depending on recommendations)
  • Protect the scar from sun and sea water for 6 months
  • Apply scar ointment no earlier than 3 weeks after suture removal
  • Scar massage and use of a compression dressing no earlier than 3 weeks after suture removal
  • Laser treatment on the scar is possible after complete wound healing (red scar stage)

Recovery time:

Sutures are removed after 5–10 (sometimes 14) days, depending on the location. Return to work is possible depending on the nature of the work and the location of the operation.
