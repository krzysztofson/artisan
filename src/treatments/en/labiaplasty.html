--- id: zmniejszenie-warg-sromowych lead: Enlarged labia minora can be a source of insecurity for many women. Thanks to this procedure performed under local anaesthesia, this intimate concern can be resolved quickly and effectively. ---

Indications:

  • Cosmetic — at the patient's request.

Before the surgery:

A consultation with a plastic surgeon is necessary, during which the specialist answers all the patient's questions and discusses the details of the procedure. Tests for local anaesthesia must be performed. In addition, blood-thinning medications (e.g. aspirin) must not be taken for at least 10 days before the procedure.

Type of anesthesia:

Local.

Duration of the surgery:

Approx. 1 hour.

Course of the surgery:

Determining the size of the future labia minora, planning the incisions, local anaesthesia, reduction of the labia minora, bleeding control and placement of absorbable sutures.

Postoperative recommendations / complications:

Protection of the operated area from injury, wound cleansing. Good hygiene is recommended.

Recovery time:

7–10 days.
