It can be used as a supporting factor before and after liposuction, as well as a standalone method for body contouring. Endermologie is particularly recommended for eliminating locally fatty areas (i.e. those that are difficult to sculpt naturally through exercise and diet). ---

How does the procedure work?

The procedure is performed using a device equipped with two independently operating massage rollers that work in combination with negative pressure. The vacuum creates a skin fold that is rolled up by the first roller and released by the second. The rollers inside the handpiece rotate around their own axes and relative to each other, in accordance with the tissue condition and the intensity of the selected treatment.

The patented Lipomassage™ technique activates the fat-release process (lipolysis), eliminates local deposits and imperfections, leading to firming and smoothing of the skin. The mechanical action of the LPG roll triggers the body's biological response: it reactivates fat release and resumes collagen and elastin production.

Lipomassage™ is recommended in treatment series. The best results are achieved after a series of 15 treatments. Effects are visible after just 3.

Benefits of endermologie – LPG Lipomassage™:

  • Firming – deep skin densification through activation of collagen and elastin production
  • Smoother skin – breaking down fat deposits, smoothing cellulite, increasing skin tone, reducing the visibility of stretch marks
  • Body contouring – endermologie enables slimming of the figure and targeted contouring in areas particularly resistant to weight loss (e.g. ankles, knees, buttocks, hips, thighs, arms, back, abdomen)
  • Feeling of lightness – improved blood and lymph circulation, elimination of heavy legs
  • Relaxation – muscle relaxation after physical exertion
  • Toxin removal – body cleansing through lymphatic drainage

Indications for endermologie treatment:

Excess adipose tissue, local fat deposits, cellulite, loss of skin elasticity, blood and lymph stasis, feeling of heavy legs

Before the procedure:

A consultation is required to rule out contraindications

Type of anesthesia:

None / not required

Duration of the endermologie procedure:

Depending on the area, approximately 35 minutes on average

Course of the endermologie procedure:

During the treatment a specially designed bodysuit must be worn. The massage device is then applied to the prepared body

Post-procedure recommendations:

To maintain the effects of endermologie after a series of treatments, 1 session per month is recommended, along with adherence to a healthy diet and light exercise

Recovery time:

Immediate; occasional mild bruising (rare, as the treatment is adjusted to skin sensitivity)
