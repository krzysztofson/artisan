The result of the procedure is a more youthful appearance, improved facial contour and restoration of tissue volume in the desired areas. ---

The procedure involves deep subcutaneous injection of an appropriate volume of filler, resulting in improved volume and a more youthful facial appearance. The procedure is performed without anaesthesia or with EMLA topical anaesthetic. Any post-procedure swelling may persist for 1-2 days.

Results after facial volumetry with hyaluronic acid

The result of facial volumetry lasts approximately 2 years.
