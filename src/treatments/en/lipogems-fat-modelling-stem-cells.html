Lipogems® stem cells – Dr Witwicki on the innovative method:

The Lipogems® procedure is intended for middle-aged patients who have experienced a reduction in adipose tissue volume, as well as slight tissue descent and loss of tone. The result of the procedure is a younger appearance, improved facial contour and restored tissue volume in the desired areas.

Applications of Lipogems®

The Lipogems® method can be used not only on the face but also for breast augmentation, buttock augmentation, calf modelling and rejuvenation of the backs of the hands. Due to its high stem cell content, which improves tissue blood supply, this method can also be used in difficult-to-heal wounds.

The Lipogems® procedure involves harvesting adipose tissue from a selected area and, after brief preparation, injecting it into the subcutaneous tissue. It improves blood supply, nourishes, has anti-inflammatory effects and adds volume. The procedure can be performed under local anaesthesia or, for more sensitive patients, intravenous sedation. Any post-procedure swelling is minimal and may persist for up to 48 hours. One of the main advantages of the Lipogems® method is its minimally invasive nature and the immediate, long-lasting rejuvenating effect and restoration of tissue volume in the desired areas.

Sample result of a Lipogems® procedure

Lipogems® is an innovative method of obtaining adipose tissue with a high stem cell content, allowing it to be administered like a filler. For the patient this means minimal post-operative swelling, improved volume, reduction of skin inflammation, revitalisation and improved circulation. We were the first in Poland to perform Lipogems® procedures in aesthetic medicine.

 

Advantages of Lipogems®

  • No swelling or bruising

  • No centrifuge or enzymes required
