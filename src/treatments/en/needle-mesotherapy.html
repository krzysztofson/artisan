Mesotherapy is a method involving a series of micro-injections, through which appropriately selected nourishing and therapeutic ingredients are introduced into the skin (hyaluronic acid, amino acids, vitamins, DMAE, caffeine – depending on the goal we want to achieve).

See also: Redermalization – more than mesotherapy – a new anti-aging treatment

Indications for needle mesotherapy:

  • nourishment and skin revitalisation of the face and body
  • wrinkle reduction
  • stretch mark reduction
  • cellulite reduction
  • minimising under-eye bags and dark circles
  • elimination of discolorations
  • stimulation of hair growth and reduction of hair loss

Why choose needle mesotherapy?

Needle mesotherapy is a very effective treatment because the injected preparation reaches the deep layers of the skin, acting strongly upon it. A series of numerous, fine punctures significantly intensify the skin's regenerative processes by stimulating fibroblasts, resulting in the formation of new collagen.

Treatment process / mesotherapy

During the procedure the patient may experience slight discomfort, so for greater comfort we apply local anaesthesia in the form of a cream.

To achieve the optimal aesthetic result, mesotherapy is usually repeated in a series of several treatments at 2–3 week intervals, 1–2 times per year. Regular mesotherapy sessions guarantee long-lasting healthy-looking skin.

Contraindications: allergies, viral hepatitis, blood diseases, pregnancy/breastfeeding, use of blood-thinning medications (e.g. aspirin), uncontrolled diabetes, cancer, skin inflammations, herpes, active acne, epilepsy.
