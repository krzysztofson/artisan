--- id: wszczepienie-implantu-w-twarz lead: This procedure is intended for patients who require augmentation of bone or tissue volume, most commonly in the area of the zygomatic arches, the base of the nosee or the chin. ---

Indications for the procedure:

  • unfavourable facial profile (a chin implant may be the solution),
  • facial tissue deficiency following trauma or caused by tissue underdevelopment.

Before surgery:

A pre-operative consultation is essential, during which the patient is informed about the necessary incisions, scars and the expected result.

A series of tests must also be performed before the surgery itself.

Type of anaesthesia:

General (recommended) or local

Duration of surgery:

1.5 to 2 hours.

Surgical procedure:

A pocket is created for the implant placement, and it is secured with sutures or tissues. The wound is then closed.

Post-operative recommendations:

When an intraoral approach is used, a liquid and pureed diet is recommended. After each meal, the oral cavity should be rinsed with an antiseptic, anti-inflammatory solution. Trauma and tissue massage should be avoided for up to 7 days after the procedure. Depending on the case, sutures are either absorbable or must be removed after one week.

Recovery time:

Bruising and swelling persist for approximately 2 weeks. The final result is visible after 4-6 weeks.
