Who is the procedure for?

The hyaluronic acid treatment is intended for people who want to improve the appearance of their face by reducing static wrinkles or shaping/enhancing the lips. The effect of smooth skin or increased volume is visible immediately. Depending on the preparation and individual conditions, it lasts approximately 8–10 months.

How to prepare for the procedure?

Please arrive for the procedure without makeup. We recommend discontinuing blood-thinning medications approximately 10 days before the planned intervention.
