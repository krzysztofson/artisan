--- id: powiekszenie-ust-kwasem-hialuronowym lead: Lip augmentation with hyaluronic acid is designed for those who wish to enhance and accentuate their lips. The main advantages of this lip augmentation method are predictable results, fast recovery and minimal invasiveness of the procedure. ---

The procedure is performed under dental anaesthesia, allowing for painless injection of the appropriate volume of filler. Hyaluronic acid can be used to model volume, contour, as well as improve lip tissue hydration.

How to prepare for lip augmentation?

Please arrive for the procedure without makeup. We recommend discontinuing blood-thinning medications approximately 10 days before the planned procedure.
