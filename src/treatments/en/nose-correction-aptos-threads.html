As a rule, Aptos threads are recommended to prevent ptosis, i.e., tissue sagging (anti-ptosis, hence the name Aptos). Using threads, the tip of the nose can also be corrected. ---

Nose Correction with Aptos Threads – Indications:

  • Drooping nasal tip
  • Small nasolabial angle
  • Nose with a small hump
  • Deviated nasal tip

Before nose correction with threads:

A consultation is necessary, during which the patient is informed about the planned implantation sites, the thread path, and the expected outcome. The patient should attend the consultation with a profile photo and an en-face photo to plan the desired nose shape. Required tests (for local anesthesia): blood type, complete blood count, coagulation panel, HBs, HCV.

Type of anesthesia:

Local

Duration of the surgery:

Approx. 1 hour.

Course of the surgery:

Under local anesthesia, the nasal tip is corrected using a special Aptos thread with a needle (non-absorbable), which maintains the effect for many years.

Postoperative recommendations:

Avoid trauma and pulling of the nose for 3–4 weeks. No heating / sun exposure for approx. 4 months.

Recovery time after nose correction:

The final result is visible after approx. 1 month. Swelling may persist for up to 3–4 weeks.
