Lipogems® is a non-enzymatic technology designed to process varying quantities of lipoaspirates, yielding non-expanded adipose tissue containing ADSC (adipose-derived stem cells). Lipogems® is a fully closed system that preserves the vascular stroma — unlike other centrifugation techniques — formed by capillaries situated between the fat cells and stromal segments containing blood vessels. Importantly, the viability of stem cells obtained using this technology exceeds 90%.

The procedure is intended for patients who have experienced a reduction in adipose tissue volume, as well as slight tissue descent and loss of skin tone. The result of the procedure is a younger, more rested appearance, improved facial contour, and restored tissue volume in the desired areas.
