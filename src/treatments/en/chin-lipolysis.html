What does the procedure involve?

The procedure most commonly involves multiple injections of a substance that destroys fat cells. The application of the preparation causes the breakdown of fat cells, inflammation and slight swelling. In cases of significant tissue excess and lax skin, additional surgical procedures (tissue tightening) may be necessary.
