--- id: korekcja-nosa-kwasem-hialuronowym lead: This method of nosee correction is designed for individuals interested in a minor correction to add volume, as well as to lift sagging tissues - such as the nasal tip or the nasofrontal angle. The advantage of nosee correction with hyaluronic acid is its minimal invasiveness and the ability to reshape the nosee. ---

The procedure is performed without anesthesia or with topical anesthesia. It involves injecting the appropriate volume of hyaluronic acid into the area requiring volume augmentation.
