For the patient, this means minimal post-operative swelling, improved volume, reduction of skin inflammation, revitalisation and improved circulation. We were the first in Poland to perform Lipogems® procedures in aesthetic medicine. Various areas can undergo revitalisation and restoration of tone and volume: facial skin, décolletage, breasts or the backs of the hands. The procedure involves harvesting adipose tissue from a selected area and, after brief preparation, implementing it into the subcutaneous tissue. The Lipogems® procedure improves blood supply, nourishes, has anti-inflammatory effects and adds volume. The procedure can be performed under local anaesthesia or, for more sensitive patients, intravenous sedation. Any post-procedure swelling is usually minimal and may persist for up to 48 hours. One of the main advantages of the Lipogems® method is its minimally invasive nature and immediate, long-lasting effect.' ---
