--- id: plastyka-lydek-powiekszenie-implantami lead: Calf augmentation using implants is a surgical procedure aimed at improving the shape and proportions of the calves. It allows correction of asymmetry, underdevelopment or post-traumatic deformities, providing a natural and aesthetic result. The procedure is performed under local or intravenous anaesthesia, and recovery usually takes approximately 7–10 days. ---

Indications:

  • Asymmetry
  • Underdevelopment or post-traumatic shape disorders

Before the surgery:

A consultation with a plastic surgeon is necessary, during which the specialist answers all the patient's questions and discusses the details of the procedure (selection of the type and size of the implant/implants). Tests for local anaesthesia must be performed. In addition, blood-thinning medications (e.g. aspirin) must not be taken for at least 10 days before the procedure.

Type of anesthesia:

Local or intravenous

Duration of the surgery:

Approx. 1 hour.

Course of the surgery:

An incision is made in the popliteal fossa. A pocket is created and the previously selected implant is inserted. Bleeding control is performed, layered sutures and a compression dressing are applied.

Postoperative recommendations:

Elevation of the lower limbs for several days after the procedure is recommended until swelling subsides. Slow mobilisation. Use of scar ointments.

Recovery time:

7–10 days.
