It also enables the absorption of active ingredients in the applied preparations with moisturising, nourishing and firming properties (amino acids, peptides, antioxidants, vitamins, microelements). ---

Effects of microPen microneedle mesotherapy:

  • rejuvenation of the skin of the face, décolletage, neck and hands
  • improvement of skin density and elasticity
  • evening out skin tone
  • reduction of discolorations
  • reduction of scars and stretch marks
  • firming of the abdomen, buttocks, bust and arms
  • improvement of the appearance of post-acne and post-burn scars

Safety:

The procedure is completely safe thanks to the use of single-use tips.

Treatment process:

The microPen treatment takes approximately 30–45 minutes. During the procedure, microneedles the diameter of a human hair make thousands of fine punctures through which a cocktail of active substances, selected by a professional, is introduced.

Note: During the procedure, skin redness is visible, with the possibility of minor bleeding. Immediately after the procedure the patient may feel warmth, a slight burning sensation and increased skin sensitivity.

Post-procedure recommendations:

  • after the procedure you can return to everyday activities (despite e.g. skin redness, makeup can be applied)
  • after 3–4 days, delicate fine-flake peeling may appear (do not touch or scratch)
  • applying special post-procedure cosmetics to the treated areas is recommended in order to reduce swelling
  • direct sun exposure and solarium must be completely avoided for 6 weeks after the procedure

For the most effective skin renewal, 3 to 5 treatments every 2–3 weeks are recommended. To maintain the effect, 2–3 full sessions per year are advised.

Contraindications:

  • active herpes
  • skin inflammations
  • infections
  • any infectious diseases
  • active acne
  • cancer
  • pregnancy and breastfeeding
