--- id: botox-leczenie-bolow-migrenowych lead: We offer migraine pain treatment. The effect of the treatment (injection of sensitive points with Botox) lasts several months. A prerequisite for undergoing the procedure is an accurate diagnosis of the condition. ---

The treatment involves subcutaneous injection of sensitive points on the forehead, occipital area, temples and neck with botulinum toxin (Botox or derivatives). The result of the treatment appears after 2–7 days and consists of interruption or reduction of the experienced symptoms for a period of 3–4 months.

Indications:

Recurrent migraine headaches

Before the procedure:

Accurate diagnosis of the cause of pain by a neurologist is recommended

Duration of the procedure:

Approx. 30 min

Side effects:

Minor bruising may occur. Otherwise the procedure carries no significant risks.
